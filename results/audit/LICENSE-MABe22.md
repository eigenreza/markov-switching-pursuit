# Files in this folder derived from MABe22

The following files contain results derived, in whole or in part, from the MABe22 mouse-triplet
recordings. The other files in this folder are not derived from MABe22 and fall under the
MIT licence of the repository.

- mabe22.json

Source: Sun J, Marks M, Geuther B, Kumar V, Robie A, Schretter C, Sheppard K, Chakraborty D,
Wagner J, Parker J, Branson K, Kennedy A. Dataset for MABe22. CaltechDATA,
doi:10.22002/rdsa8-rde65. Described in Sun JJ et al., MABe22: a multi-species multi-task
benchmark for learned representations of behavior, arXiv:2207.10553.
